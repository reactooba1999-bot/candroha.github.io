About Candroha International University

Established in 1961, Candroha International University (CIU) has been a beacon of academic excellence, research, and innovation. Located in the United Kingdom, CIU is dedicated to fostering intellectual growth, interdisciplinary learning, and global knowledge exchange.

Our university is committed to preparing future leaders through rigorous academic programs, cutting-edge research, and a vibrant campus community. We believe in the power of education to transform lives and shape a better world.
